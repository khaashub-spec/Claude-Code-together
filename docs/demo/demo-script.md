# Demo Script: "A Day at Sunrise Auto" (about 10 minutes)

Goal: show a dealer that every customer gets an instant, helpful reply on any channel, nothing falls through the cracks, and customers are followed up after the sale.

Before the demo, import the data in `sunrise-auto-vehicles.csv` and `sunrise-auto-contacts.csv`, and set up the sub-account using `demo-build-checklist.md`. Use two phones: Phone A is the "customer", and Phone B is the "salesperson" with the GHL mobile app.

All names, vehicles and numbers are fictional.

---

## Opening line (30 seconds)

"Most dealers lose customers in the first few minutes: a missed call, a slow reply, or no follow-up after the sale. I'll show you a day at a dealership where that doesn't happen."

## Scene 1: Missed call (1 minute)

**Do:** From Phone A, call the demo number and let it ring out (or hang up).
**Show:** A text arrives within seconds: "Hi, this is Sunrise Auto. Sorry we missed your call! Are you looking at a specific vehicle, or can I help with service?"
**Say:** "Every missed call becomes a conversation instead of a lost customer."
**Reply as customer:** "Do you have a 2020 Civic?"
**Expect:** Hala answers with the Civic EX (SA1001, $18,995, 41,200 miles, silver) and asks about a test drive.

## Scene 2: Text to booked test drive (2 minutes)

**Customer messages:**
1. "Is the Civic still available?"
2. "Can I see it Saturday morning?"
3. "10am works."

**Expect:** Hala offers open slots, books a Test Drive, and sends a confirmation.
**Show:** In GHL, the appointment appears on the Test Drive calendar, the contact is created in the Sales pipeline at "Appointment Set", and the assigned salesperson gets a task.
**Say:** "That happened at 9pm with nobody in the office."

## Scene 3: Price, trade-in and financing handled safely (1.5 minutes)

**Customer messages:**
1. "What's your best price on the RAV4?" (expect: listed price only, handoff to salesperson)
2. "I have a 2015 Corolla to trade in. What's it worth?" (expect: collects details and offers an Appraisal, no value quoted)
3. "Can I get financing with bad credit?" (expect: general answer, no promises, offers a Finance Consult)

**Say:** "Hala never promises a price, an approval or a trade-in value. It books the person who can."

## Scene 4: Phone call with Voice AI (1.5 minutes)

**Do:** Call the demo number during the demo (or after hours) and ask: "Do you have any SUVs under $25,000, and can I come in tomorrow?"
**Expect:** Voice AI describes available options (CR-V, Santa Fe is sold so not offered, Sportage, Escape is on hold so not offered), then offers to book.
**Say:** "The same assistant works on the phone, in text, on web chat and social messages."

## Scene 5: Manager view (1 minute)

**Show on a computer:**
- The Sales pipeline: new leads, appointments, showed, negotiating.
- Lead source and response time on a lead.
- The "no response in 5 minutes" alert for the manager.
- Dashboard: leads by source, response time, appointments.

**Say:** "A manager sees what's happening and who's falling behind without asking anyone."

## Scene 6: Team on the go (45 seconds)

**Show on Phone B:** The mobile app with new conversation notifications, a task to call Maya Johnson, and the ability to reply from the phone.
**Say:** "Your salespeople reply from the lot. With the white-label option, this is an app with your dealership's name on it."

## Scene 7: After the sale, where customers are kept (2 minutes)

**Show:**
1. **Grace Turner** (bought a Santa Fe 3 days ago): thank-you text sent, review request ready.
2. **Omar Hassan** (30 days): the check-in text, and what happens when he replies "not happy" (manager alert, no public review request).
3. **Fatima Ali**: service due date approaching, so a reminder goes out and she can book by text.
4. **Samuel Green**: declined tires last visit, so he appears on the declined-work list with a follow-up.
5. **Victor Lopez**: lease ends December 15, so an upgrade offer goes out at 60 days.

**Say:** "Selling the car is one sale. Keeping the customer is the next five."

## Close (30 seconds)

"What you saw: every inquiry answered instantly, appointments booked automatically, your team alerted, and customers followed up after the sale. It all runs inside one system. What would matter most for your dealership?"

## Backup lines for tough questions

- **"Does it work with our DMS?"** "We can import your customer and service data today, and we're looking at direct connections for the main DMS systems. Which one do you use?"
- **"Will customers know it's AI?"** "It identifies itself as the dealership's virtual assistant if asked, and hands off to your team whenever a human is needed."
- **"What about texting rules?"** "We register your messaging for carrier compliance and honour opt-outs automatically."
- **"What does it cost?"** Quote only the packages you have decided; don't improvise.
